## Current bottleneck
**Export / customs clearance out of Brazil via Black King's personal CNPJ.** Black King's CNPJ went **INAPTA on 2026-06-08** (omissão de declarações — 2+ years of missed DCTF/ECF/ECD/DAS), plus expired e-CNPJ and no commerce CNAE (service CNAE only; no IE; no NF-e model 55). Until cleared (CNAE 46.23-1/04 + IE + NF-e credentialing at SEFAZ-BA) **no export NF-e can be issued** — this blocks every channel routed through Black King. See `briefs/BRAZIL_EXPORT_ENTITY_BRIEF.md` for why the personal CNPJ doesn't scale and `BRAZIL_EXPORT_LANE_LEARNINGS.md` §2 for the 7 concurrent issues.

**Freight-forwarder discovery:** a trading company was found for BR→US customs clearance, but (a) pricing looks off, and (b) it is **unconfirmed whether that trading company is GACC-registered** — a separate, additional requirement for a **China-bound** route vs a US-bound one. Structural rule (learnings §1/§5): **MAPA/GACC attach to the production enterprise (the facility that processes/benefits beans), NOT the exporter/trader.** A "por conta e ordem" trading-company bridge (fee 0.5–2% FOB, IN RFB 1.861/2018) ships under the producer's registration — the trading company itself does not need its own GACC, but the producer's registration is the one presented at Chinese customs. **Verify with the freight forwarder + Ling before relying on it for the China lane.**
