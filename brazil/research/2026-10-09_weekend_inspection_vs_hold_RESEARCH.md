# RESEARCH — Weekend inspection vs. weekend hold (SFO, agriculture/FDA)

**Date:** 2026-10-09 (Friday) · **Author:** Sophia Truesight (autopilot) · thread 10800
**Question (governor):** *"Do you think they do inspection during weekend or they hold it over the weekend?"*

---

## 0. Short answer

> **They hold it.**

For a **non-perishable** shipment the governing rule is explicit:

> *"For non-perishable shipments, inspection appointments are **not available**. Clearance will occur
> during regular CBP operating hours."*

Only **perishables** are eligible for scheduled examination outside 07:00–19:00, and only via
**reimbursable overtime** that must be requested by **2:00 PM the preceding Friday**.

Our cocoa is **non-perishable**. And **Monday 12/10 is Columbus Day**, a federal holiday on which
*"cargo release and trade processing offices will be closed."*

⇒ **Realistic resumption of movement: Tuesday 13/10.**

## 1. The trap — CBP publishes two SFO hour sets and they disagree

| Facility | Published hours | What it actually governs |
|---|---|---|
| **Air Cargo (Trade) — San Francisco** | **8:00AM – 11:30PM (Pacific), Monday – Friday** | ⬅ **our cargo desk. WEEKDAYS ONLY.** |
| San Francisco International Airport (SFO) | 6:00AM – 12:00AM (Pacific), **Sunday – Saturday** | passengers — **not** cargo |
| Entrance & clearance inspection (KSFO) | **7 days/week**, 0800–2200 (*wheels down*) | **aircraft**, not consignments |

The 24/7 impression comes from the **passenger** and **aircraft** rows. **Freight clearance is Mon–Fri.**
Reading the wrong row is how this gets answered wrongly.

## 4. Reimbursable overtime — the machine, and its deadline

CBP *can* work outside core agriculture hours, but only as a **billed, pre-requested** service:

| Service timing | Request deadline |
|---|---|
| Weekday (Mon–Fri) | 2:00 PM **day of service** |
| **Weekend (Sat–Sun)** | 2:00 PM **preceding Friday** |
| **Monday federal holiday** | 2:00 PM **preceding Friday** |
| Weekday federal holiday | 2:00 PM **day prior** |

⇒ To have been worked **this weekend or Columbus Day**, the request had to be filed by **2:00 PM today**.

**Mechanics:** filed via **CBPLink** (`cbplink.cbp.dhs.gov`); requires a **CBP Form 5106** on file for
the billable party; **Importer ID/Tax ID of the billable party** must be supplied; billed by CBP's
**Revenue Division** — *"local ports cannot provide cost estimates, generate bills, or accept payment."*

7 CFR Part 354 governs the underlying authority and requires the request be made *"sufficiently in
advance"*.

## 5. Two things worth keeping in perspective

1. **A hold is not a finding.** We still have **no emergency action notice**, no Notice of FDA Action,
   no CBP message. Per APHIS: *"The hold may likely mean that CBP hasn't gotten to it yet, and it could
   be temporary."* An EAN issues only if a problem is **confirmed**. **We are in a queue, not in a
   violation.**
2. **The holiday does NOT burn our response window.** FDA's clock is **10 business days** from the
   Notice of Detention, and the RPM counts *"10 days, **excluding Saturday, Sunday, and Holiday**."*
   ⇒ The four-day gap costs us **storage and calendar time — not our legal response window.**
