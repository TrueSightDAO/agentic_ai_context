## Keep in mind for the future

- **The 6-month inactivity rule is a standing trap.** A SISCOMEX/RADAR habilitação silently drops after
  ~6 months with no movement. During any shipping programme, **log a movement (even administrative)** to
  keep it alive, or diarize the expiry. Do not let it idle across a season.
- **Single-certificate / single-machine risk.** A Brazilian-entity operation keyed to one person's
  A1 certificate on one laptop is a single point of failure. Keep **more than one certificate-capable
  device and custodian** for Black King (and record where the certificate is installed).
