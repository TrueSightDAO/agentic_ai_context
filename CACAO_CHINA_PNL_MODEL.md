## 4. Active Ingredient — Epicatechin (表儿茶素) & Stem Cell Science

### The Key Compound
The specific molecule responsible for doubling circulating stem cells is **(−)-epicatechin**, a flavanol unique to cacao.

### Clinical Evidence
- **UCSF — JACC (2010):** 16 coronary artery disease patients, 375mg flavanols twice daily × 30 days → **2× circulating stem cells** + 47% improved blood vessel function
- **Complutense — PubMed (2020):** Confirmed epicatechin alone is the active agent protecting endothelial cells
- **Urbino — PMC (2024):** Cocoa polyphenols protect muscle stem cell mitochondria + double mitochondrial count

### Mechanism
Epicatechin upregulates nitric oxide (NO) in the endothelium → signals bone marrow to release endothelial progenitor cells (stem cells) → repairs blood vessels.

### Commercial Epicatechin Products (benchmark)

|---|---|---|

| CocoaVia Ultra 750mg | ~135mg | $45-55/bottle |
| CCV-3® (advanced) | ~600mg | $60+/bottle |

### Our Dual-Product Opportunity
Since cacao shell is our raw material (currently a waste byproduct):
| Product | Processing | Epicatechin | Price |
