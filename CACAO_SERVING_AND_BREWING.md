# Cacao Serving & Brewing Ratios

**Status:** living document · last updated 2026-11-26
**Source:** Gary Teh, given to Cristiano ahead of serving a prospective-customer group

---

## Why this exists

The DAO has detailed runbooks for supply, freight, repacking and QR lineage — but nothing on **how to actually prepare and serve the cacao**. Until now every "what ratio do I use?" question routed to Gary personally. This records the answer so it can be handed to a host, a partner, or a retailer without a round trip.

---

## Method 1 — House ratio (default)

By **parts**, not absolute units. Any consistent measure works (grams, cups, scoops).

| Component | Parts |
|---|---|
| **Cacao nibs** | **2** |
| **Oat milk** | **4** |
| **Water** | **4** |
| Sugar *(optional)* | **0.5** |

### Worked example

Using 50 g as one part:

| Component | Parts | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Cacao nibs | 2 | 100 g |
| Oat milk | 4 | 200 ml |
| Water | 4 | 200 ml |
| Sugar (optional) | 0.5 | 25 g |

Yield: ~500 ml finished drink from 100 g nibs.

### Notes

- **Sugar is optional and additive, not substitutive** — 0.5 part is a guideline for a lightly sweetened serve. Omit for a pure cacao experience; the drink is not bitter at this ratio because the oat milk carries it.
- **Ratio, not recipe.** Halve or double freely; only the proportions matter.

---

## Method 2 — The Italian way (nibs as coffee beans)

Treat the cacao nibs **as if they were coffee beans** — grind and brew in the manner you would coffee.

**Expect a different drink:**

- **Thinner body** than Method 1 — no milk solids to carry it
- **Reads much more like coffee** in the cup
- **Slightly different taste profile** — cacao, but positioned where a coffee drinker's palate expects coffee

### When to choose which

| Situation | Method |
|---|---|
| Serving as a cacao *beverage* / ceremonial context | **1 — House ratio** |
| Serving to coffee drinkers, or as a coffee substitute | **2 — Italian** |
| Audience unfamiliar with cacao, needs a familiar anchor | **2 — Italian** |
| Want the richest, most forgiving result | **1 — House ratio** |

---

## Why oat milk

Oat milk is the specified milk. It carries cacao's flavour without the acidity and heaviness dairy introduces, and it froths acceptably. Where oat milk is unavailable, a neutral plant milk is the closest substitute; dairy will work but changes the profile noticeably.

---

## Caveats

- These are **proportions for a good serve**, not a lab-verified extraction optimum. Nobody has measured yield, particle size or extraction time.
- The **Italian method** summary above derives from the upload-time parse of a dark-mode screenshot whose body text did not OCR cleanly (tesseract psm 4/6/11 and a 2× crop all failed to recover the paragraph). It is faithful to what was read, but it is **not** a verbatim transcript — treat as paraphrase.
- No variant of this has been tried with the DAO's whole-bean or nib stock at production scale.
