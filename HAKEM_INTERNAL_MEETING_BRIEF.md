## Framing to lead with

Per Elizabeth, the supply chain story is the product. Three words carry it:

- **Regenerative** — cabruca and agroforestry systems, cacao under intact canopy, not cleared land.
- **Transformative** — what the premium does to the farmer's economics, not just what the bean contains.
- **Transparent** — per-bag lineage that resolves to a named farm. This is our strongest real asset and it is already running.

Then: how we blueprint cacao — the engineering discipline behind the sourcing. Define the spec, measure against it, publish the result, drop what fails. Lead with the method, not the certificate.

## The one conflict to resolve before this language is used

Elizabeth suggests foregrounding *individually batch tested*. Today that is not a description of us — it is a claim about a capability we have not exercised. No panel has been run on any of our beans. No measured flavanol figure exists.

Three ways to handle it, in order of preference:
1. **Make it true first.** Run Phase 1 — one lot per biome, one accredited panel each.
2. **Say what is true today.** "Every bag is individually traceable to a named farm and lot."
3. **Say it as intent.** "Built to be individually batch tested — the panel is the next step."

Do not say it plainly in the meeting. If Hakem asks what we have tested, the answer is nothing yet.

## Positioning angles from Elizabeth

**Max activeness** — needs a working definition before it is said aloud (total flavanols? epicatechin? DP 1–10?). Theme, not a claim, until measured.

**Coffee replacement / sustained energy drink** — strong angle, but must acknowledge theobromine/caffeine as a real dosing constraint for the exact buyer this targets. Position as lower-acid and theobromine-forward, never caffeine-free.

**One tree planted for each sale** — verify before stating as a blanket claim. Tree-planting runs through the SunMint program (QR-linked, shipment- and plot-specific, with real capacity limits and liability accounting — "Cacao Tree Purchased – Not Planted" is tracked as an open liability, not an instant fact). Not, today, a universal "every unit sold plants one tree" mechanism. Say what's true today instead: "every tree-planting event is QR-traceable to a specific plot."

**ESG play** — true as category fit, not as a certification. No third-party ESG rating, no verified carbon/biodiversity credit, no audited impact report exists today.

## What we do not have — do not overstate

- No analytical panel run; no measured flavanol figure.
- Concentrated extract under discussion only — not scoped, not tested, no cost/kg.
- Coopercabruca converts/exports its own membership's beans only — cannot absorb independent warehouse stock.
- CEPOTX producer rosters captured from video, unverified; one member code has an open one-digit discrepancy.
- No carbon, biodiversity, or restoration credit measured or commissioned.
- No formal ESG rating or certification.
- Tree-planting is not a verified one-for-one guarantee across every sale.
- Cadmium risk unmeasured; Brazil-specific data thin.
- A lab certificate proves composition, not efficacy.

## The one question that decides the conversation

The partnership claims 10,000 hectares. We claim 10,000 hectares. Same acres, adjacent acres, or unrelated geography?

- **Same acres** — a merge conversation.
- **Adjacent** — a joint programme with a shared measurement standard.
- **Unrelated** — a technology or supply conversation, not a land partnership.

Ask this early.

## Questions to ask

1. Which specific estates and municipalities sit inside their 10,000 hectares? Can they map them?
2. What does their QR traceability record today — per estate, per lot, or per bag? Who runs the ledger?
3. Have any of their lots been through an accredited panel? Which lab, which analytes, can we see a certificate?
4. Who holds export rights and Brazilian registrations on their beans?
5. Who funds the restoration, and what does the funder require as proof?
6. What would they want from us that they cannot build themselves?

## Four structures worth scoping

| Structure | What it looks like | Our leverage |
|---|---|---|
| Lineage layer | They adopt our per-unit QR and ledger for their estates | Strong — already built |
| Joint measurement | Pool one lot per biome, one panel, shared certificate of analysis | Strong — splits cost, doubles credibility |
| Supply lane | They route export-ready lots through our cooperative relationships | Medium — depends on membership |
| Mission alignment only | Public association, no commercial terms yet | Weak but honest, buys time |

Sequencing: measurement first, structure second.
