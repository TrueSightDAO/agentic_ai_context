## Evidence limitations — recorded deliberately

**1. No EXIF on either image.** Both photos were checked programmatically; timestamps have been stripped, most likely by messaging-app transit. **The dates therefore cannot be machine-verified from metadata**, and no claim is made that they can.

**2. Photo 1 is self-evidencing; photo 2 is not.** OCR of the first image returns readable event branding:

> *"...BALL | ...WORLD IN GUANG... | Where..."*

That matches the event's official *"One Night. The World in Guangzhou." / "Where The World Comes Together"* theme — so the image is **corroborably the AmCham Winter Ball**, not merely asserted to be. The second image yields no legible text and is weaker evidence: it shows a meeting, not which meeting.

**3. Neither photo shows Rusmin identifiably.** The images evidence **attendance at the occasion**, not the identity of the counterparty. That gap is real and is stated rather than papered over — the person who can confirm the counterparty is Gary, not the photograph.
