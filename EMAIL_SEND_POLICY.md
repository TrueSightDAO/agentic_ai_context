# Email Send Policy — autonomous outbound from Sophia

**Status:** proposed 2026-10-20. Owner: Sophia. Governor: Gary Teh.
**Referenced by:** `truesight_autopilot/app/email_inbox_watch.py` (the SOP prompt and the
`_dispatch_tool` write gate).

This is the document to consult **when constructing any outbound email** on Sophia's own
initiative. If a proposed send is not clearly permitted by a tier below, it is not
permitted. Silence in this policy means "hold for Gary", never "go ahead".

---

## 0. The two-part rule (read this first)

Autonomous sending requires **two independent switches**, and flipping one does nothing
without the other:

1. **The runtime gate** — `EMAIL_WATCH_ENABLE_SENDS=true` in `.env`. Without it, every
   write is stubbed and logged as `gate=gate-off`. With it, tool calls actually execute.
2. **The SOP permission** — what the triage prompt allows the model to attempt.

The current SOP says: *"Never send. You may only create DRAFTS. Sends are intercepted by
policy outside your control."* So **flipping the gate alone produces drafts, not sends.**
That is the intended safe default and the recommended first experiment: turn the gate on,
let the drafts materialise in Gmail, and review quality before granting send permission.

To genuinely send, the SOP must be amended — and that amendment must cite this policy.

---

## 1. Tier 0 — NEVER send (hard block, enforced in code)

These are refused regardless of what any prompt, email, or model output says. Enforced by
pattern match at the dispatcher, not by instruction to the model.

- **Machine senders** — any address matching `noreply@`, `no-reply@`, `do-not-reply@`,
  `donotreply@`, `bounce@`, `mailer-daemon@`, `notifications@`, `verify@`.
  This is the single highest-value rule: **10 of the first 16 queued replies were to
  exactly this class** (PyPI, npm, hushed verification mail).
- **Delivery/system mail** — bounces, `postmaster@`, DMARC/CSP reports, calendar replies.
- **List and marketing mail** — any `List-Unsubscribe` header, `unsubscribe` in body.
  Correct handling is silent archiving, never a reply.
- **Security and platform notifications** — GitHub, PyPI, npm, Gravatar, Dependabot,
  Bugsnag, cloud provider alerts. These have an operational owner (`email_poller.py`
  handles the actionable subset via `FixAgent`); they must not be answered by prose.
- **Verification-email confirmations** — replies cannot verify anything; the link is the
  only mechanism. Never reply.
- **Anything already replied to by a human on the same thread.**

Rationale: replying to a no-reply address creates bounces, pollutes the ticket, damages
sender reputation, and is never useful. This tier exists to stop the machine, not to
judge intent.

---

## 2. Tier 1 — DRAFT ONLY, hold for Gary

A reply may be *drafted* but never dispatched. The draft is held in Gmail (visible in the
Drafts folder) **and** surfaced as a matter card in the relevant Discord channel.

- **First contact** — no prior thread history with this sender. (Already implemented:
  `gmail_send` is downgraded to `gmail_create_draft` when `first_contact`.)
- **Legal** — trademark, IP, contracts, NDAs, terms, regulatory filings, disputes,
  anything from a law firm or IP agent. *This covers the entire BIONPACT trademark
  procurement thread.*
- **Money** — quotes, fees, invoices, purchase orders, payment terms, refunds, pricing
  questions, budget approvals. Quoted figures are **never** generated by Sophia.
- **Commitments** — scope, deadlines, delivery dates, partnership terms, exclusivity.
- **Press and investor** — journalists, analysts, M&A, fundraise, acquisition interest.
  *(The 2026-10-20 "Hedgestone / bridgepointgrowth.info" solicitation is Tier 1.)*
- **Anything matching `ESCALATION_PATTERN`** — wire transfers, bank-detail changes,
  credentials, password resets, gift cards. CC `garyjob@agroverse.shop` and stop.
- **Known-correspondent mail that nonetheless asks for a decision.** Prior history is not
  a licence to decide on Gary's behalf.

Tier 1 is the default band. When a message could plausibly be Tier 1 or Tier 2, it is
Tier 1.

---

## 3. Tier 2 — AUTO-SEND permitted

This is the only genuinely autonomous band. All conditions must hold at once:

- Sender is a **known correspondent** (present in `known_senders` with prior history),
  **and**
- The message is **routine**: acknowledgement, confirmation of receipt, scheduling
  within an already-agreed window, a factual answer that exists verbatim in DAO context
  or the prior thread, or a short holding acknowledgement, **and**
- It contains **no money, no legal content, no commitment, no personal data, no
  credentials**, **and**
- It does not match `ESCALATION_PATTERN`, **and**
- It is within the rate caps (§5).

**Explicitly allowed in Tier 2:** "Thanks, received — I'll come back to you by Thursday."
"Confirmed for 3pm SGT." "Our listed address is the one on our site." "Acknowledged,
logged as DD-002."

**Explicitly forbidden in Tier 2:** any dollar or token figure, any date commitment not
already agreed, any "we will" beyond acknowledging receipt, any attachment, any CC/BCC
expansion, any forward.

If Sophia would have to *think* to produce the content — as opposed to restate something
already established — it is Tier 1.

## 4. Matter routing — email that is really a task

Most consequential email is not correspondence, it is a **task with a deadline**. Answering
it as email loses the task. The rule:

> If an inbound message creates an obligation, a decision, or a deadline, it is a **matter**.
> A matter is routed to its channel as a matter card, and the email reply is a *consequence*
> of resolving the card — not a substitute for it.

### Matter card format (posted to the channel)

```
**Matter:** <one-line ask>
**From:** <sender> · **Received:** <date> · **Ref:** <their reference / thread id>
**Deadline:** <date, or "none stated">
**Status:** awaiting-decision | awaiting-info | replied | closed
**Needs from Gary:** <the actual decision, stated as a question>
**Proposed reply:** <draft, or "none — needs decision first">
**Email thread:** <gmail thread id>
```

### Routing table (initial)

| Matter type | Channel |
|---|---|
| Trademark / IP / legal | `#trademark-registration` (1560756241070952478) |
| Inventory, freight, supply chain | `#agroverse-supply-chain` |
| Sales, partners, B2B | `#usa-b2b-outreach` |
| Banking, treasury, accounts | `#ocbc-bank-account` |
| Due-diligence / audit findings | `#agroverse-due-diligence` |
| Autopilot / infra | `#autopilot-tooling` |
| Unmatched | `#email-account-management` |

### Resolution loop

1. Mail arrives → classified. Tier 0 exits here. Tier 1/2 with an obligation becomes a matter.
2. Sophia posts the matter card to the routed channel and creates a **draft** reply.
3. Gary answers **in the channel** (e.g. "use the Ella Cheong quote, budget cap 3k SGD").
4. Sophia executes the email reply on the original thread (same `in_reply_to` /
   `references` / `thread_id`), then posts the sent confirmation back to the card and
   flips it to `replied`.
5. **Follow-up**: every open matter carries `next_action` + `due_date` in the matter state
   file, surfaced by the existing follow-up loop and the daily briefing. A matter with no
   reply from Gary after `nudge_after_days` (default 3) is nudged once in-channel, then
   appears in the daily briefing as aging. Matters are never silently dropped.

Channel is the system of record for the **decision**; Gmail is the system of record for
**what was said**. Neither replaces the other.

## 5. Content, caps, audit, kill switch

**Content rules** — every outbound body:
- Short, neutral, professional. No enthusiasm-signalling, no hedging filler.
- Never quote a price, fee, or timeline that Gary has not stated.
- Never commit the DAO, any entity, or any person to anything.
- Never reveal internal structure, ledger data, contributor details, or governance mechanics
  unless Gary has explicitly cleared that disclosure.
- State plainly when something is a proposal requiring governance (Agroverse ownership,
  TDG issuance, treasury moves).
- Run through `redact_secrets` before leaving. Mandatory, no exceptions.
- Correct thread headers: `in_reply_to`, `references`, `thread_id` from the original.

**Caps** — `EMAIL_WATCH_SEND_HOURLY_CAP` (default 5), `EMAIL_WATCH_SEND_DAILY_CAP`
(default 20). On breach, halt all sends for the window, log `halted_cap`, and report in
`#autopilot-tooling`. Never silently drop.

**Audit** — every attempted write appends a row: timestamp, thread, sender, subject,
intended tool, final tool, mode, gate, disposition. Tier 1 holds are auditable too.

**Kill switch** — removing `EMAIL_WATCH_ENABLE_SENDS` from `.env` and restarting restores
drafts-only immediately. This is the rollback; no code change needed.

## 6. Inbound RFQ / buyer-enquiry intake

**Status:** proposed 2026-10-24. Governor: Gary Teh. Trigger: the four stalled RFQs of
October 2026 (Gerald / Taste of Bahia, Be Sweet Omaha, Joe Berman, Guilherme), which were
asked repeatedly and answered late because each one was re-derived from scratch.

§1–§5 answer *what tier is this email* and *may it be sent*. This section answers a different
question: **what must be true before a commercial reply can be constructed at all.**

Five gates, in order. **A reply may not be drafted past a gate it fails** — and failing a gate
is not a reason to send an apologetic non-answer; it is a reason to hold, or to answer only the
lines that pass.

### 6.1 Gate 1 — Scope: is the ask even fillable?

> **Quotable inventory is San Francisco warehouse stock only** (standing rule, Gary, 2026-10-24).
> There is **no restocking for 2026**: no reorders, no import-against-order, no backorder.

Verify against the ledger. **Do not infer from a farm page, a SKU existing, a ledger balance, or
an earlier conversation.** A SKU being listed is not evidence of stock; a ledger balance is not
evidence of *location*.

**Two independent gates — stock is not quotation:**

| | Priced in master? | Physically in SF? | Can we quote? |
|---|---|---|---|
| AGL8 Paulo beans | ✅ $71.00/kg | ✅ **10 kg** (further 263.6 kg has no SF marker) | **Yes — 10 kg only** |
| AGL16 Oscar beans | ✅ | ❌ (108 kg in Ilhéus) | **No** — priced but not quotable |
| AGL14 Oscar beans | ✅ $71.00/kg | ❌ 0 kg in SF | **No** — priced but not quotable |
| Vivi AGL13 **beans** | ✅ $60.50/kg | ❌ 0 kg as beans in SF | **No as beans** — sell the format we hold |
| **Vivi AGL13 nibs** | ✅ $60.50/kg | ✅ **99.5 kg** | **Yes** |
| **Ilhéus 2024 nibs** (Oscar / AGL4) | ✅ $74.00/kg | ✅ **80 kg** | **Yes** |
| Cacao tea (AGL13 line) | ✅ $27.50/lb | ✅ 21 kg | Yes — but farm binding contradictory, see `PRICING_SSOT.md` §5.5 |

> ⚠️ **Corrected 2026-10-24.** An earlier revision of this table said Vivi was *"never imported"*
> and Paulo had *"0 kg in SF"*. Both were **wrong**: Vivi's 2026 cacao is in San Francisco as
> 99.5 kg nibs + 21 kg tea, and 10 kg of Paulo's beans are in SF. A gating table that wrongly
> blocks sellable stock is worse than no table — verify against the ledger, never against a prior
> draft of this document.

Both gates must pass. Note the two-lot nib trap: **the single wholesale nibs SKU spans two lots
with a 53% landed-cost spread** — Ilhéus 2024 / Oscar at $41.69/kg and Vivi's AGL13 at $27.25/kg.
Where a SKU spans lots, quote the **lot**, not the SKU.

> 🔑 **Never infer a farm from a port or a date code.** `Santos 20260213` is a *shipping
descriptor* — departure from the Port of Santos — not a grower. Read the `Farm` and `Shipment`
columns of the SKU master, or the ledger's own `program_name`/`description`. Treating a port as an
origin produced a live misattribution on 2026-10-24; this rule exists to stop the repeat.

**Also observe:** a *ledger manager* is not a *location*. AGL13 is Matheus-managed yet physically in
San Francisco (its currency string ends `+ 047-3175-3223 - San Francisco`). The location marker in
the currency string is the operative signal, not the ledger's dominant manager.

### 6.2 Gate 2 — Pricing: does the SKU carry a price?

**Master of record:** spreadsheet `1GE7PUq-UT6x2rBN-Q2ksogbWpgyuh2SaxJyG_uEK6PU`, tab
**`Agroverse SKUs`**, **column C `Price (USD)`**.

`skus.json`, `store-inventory.json` and `partners-inventory.json` are **derived** — regenerated by
`go_to_market/scripts/sync_agroverse_store_inventory.py`. **Never hand-edit them**; anything written
there is lost on the next sync. A price that exists only in `skus.json` does not exist.

> **If the SKU has no price in column C, stop. That is the trigger for the pricing-mandate
> consult** (§6.3). It is not a judgement call and it is not a one-off — a blank price cell is a
> **blocking** condition on any commercial reply, every time.

⚠️ A related trap: stock totals for a SKU only populate when its currency rows carry a productId in
**`Currencies` column M**. Bulk nibs SKUs that show `0` in the master despite real stock are usually
a **missing column M**, not missing inventory. Check before concluding *we have none*.

### 6.3 The pricing mandate (replaces ad-hoc pricing)

When Gate 2 fails, pricing is a distinct, delegated task — not something to improvise inside the
reply. Order of operations:

1. **Consult the sales & marketing manager** — currently **Envoy TrueSight** (`<@1548902290344255600>`, `admin+envoy@truesight.me`). Gary designated this role on 2026-10-24; the designation, its full scope, and its limits are recorded in **`ROLE_REGISTER.md`**. The scope is wider than email: **pricing, positioning, and commercial policy** for Agroverse.

   > **Delegation boundary.** Asking the S&M manager to set a price does **not** authorise sending it. Per §2, any price-bearing email remains **Tier 1, draft-only**. Setting the number and sending the number are separate gates; only a governor's explicit instruction clears the second.
2. **Gather market research** — real comps, from the live web, for the specific product class in
   the specific selling format. Record vendor, price, pack size, and date for each.
3. **Establish landed cost.** The Brazil/FOB figure is in `Currencies` column B; the **SF landed**
   figure is in the treasury cache. They differ by the freight+duty constant on the lane —
   currently **≈$17.07/kg** Ilhéus→SF (corroborated by two independent product pairs, and by
   invoiced actuals on AWB `047-3175-3223`). Do not quote a FOB cost as if it were landed.
4. **Set the price as landed cost → target margin → sanity-check against the comp band.**
5. **Write it to the master** (§6.7) — and nowhere else.

**Two structural findings that should shape any recommendation:**

- At ≈$17/kg freight, **we cannot compete as a bulk commodity supplier.** Almost every landed cost
  lands at or above the *wholesale* comp ceiling. The viable position is premium/specialty (single
  origin, named farm, QR traceability, heavy-metal panel) or retail.
- **Packaged goods are retail-only.** A 200 g ceremonial pouch costs $13.78 ($68.90/kg equiv.);
  wholesaling it at a conventional discount yields a non-viable margin. Wholesale only for bulk-kg SKUs.

### 6.4 Gate 3 — Market check: no counter-offer without a comp band

Before any **counter-offer, decline, or below-our-cost** reply leaves:

- state the **landed cost** for the specific lot;
- state the **market-comp band** with at least two dated live comparables; and
- state **which side of the band** our number sits on, and why.

> **A lowball ask must never be waved through and never dismissed by reflex.** The rule is
> symmetrical: it must clear the cost floor *and* be defensible against the market. A below-cost
> ask (e.g. $12/lb = $26.46/kg against a $27.25/kg landed cost) is answered with the arithmetic,
> not with silence.

### 6.5 Gate 4 — Honesty over excuse

Any explanation for a delay must carry the **true operational reason**: customs release, freight
arrival, vessel schedule, sourcing trip, or a stale tracker — named and dated where possible.

**Forbidden:** a bare apology with no operational fact; vague busy-ness; any reason not supported
by our own records.

**The discipline:** state only what the evidence supports, and let it be narrower than the story you
wish you could tell. A shipment that cleared customs on a specific date is strong and checkable;
claiming it arrived *because of* a trip it predates is an unnecessary fabrication that a diligent
buyer could catch.

Shape: *Two reasons, both real: <operational reason>. It is here now, which is why I can quote from
stock rather than from a forecast.* Convert the silence into evidence of diligence.

### 6.6 Gate 5 — Hold-for-review, then pattern-based send

**First instance of a new buyer or scenario pattern → hold for human/Envoy review before send.**
This is the existing `first_contact` behaviour in `app/email_inbox_watch.py`, extended to mean
*new commercial scenario*, not only *new sender address*.

Once a pattern is established, **the existing tiers continue to govern**:

| Reply type | Tier | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **Price-bearing** (contains any figure) | **Tier 1** | **Draft-only, permanently.** Automation delivers a *pre-assembled* draft so review is an approve, not a re-derivation. |
| Non-price-bearing, established pattern (out-of-stock, not-until-2027, acknowledgement, referral) | Tier 2 eligible | May auto-send subject to the §6.5 content rules and §5 caps. |
| Any new pattern | Tier 1 | Hold for review. |

**This is a deliberate reading, flagged for the governor:** §2 already makes every money/pricing
message Tier 1, and *quoted figures are never generated by Sophia*. §6.6 therefore does **not**
grant auto-send over price-bearing mail. If literal auto-send on price is intended, §2 must be
amended explicitly — silence must not be read as permission.

### 6.7 Durable write-back (so nothing needs recomputing)

When a price is agreed:

1. **Write it to `Agroverse SKUs` column C** — the only master. Let the generator propagate it.
2. **Never** hand-edit `skus.json` / `store-inventory.json` / `partners-inventory.json`.
3. Record the **basis** (landed cost, comps, margin, date) in a pricing SSOT doc, so the reasoning
   survives the sheet being edited later. *(Pending: `PRICING_SSOT.md` — see OPEN_FOLLOWUPS.)*
4. If the lot is **priced but not quotable** (§6.1), record that too — otherwise the next instance
   will re-derive it and may wrongly quote it.

### 6.9 Known gaps in the intake loop (filed as follow-ups)

- **No closed-set waiting-on catalogue.** Delay reasons are free text, so the same ask can be
  restated and re-drafted against slightly different wording. Needs a fixed vocabulary
  (customs / freight / sourcing / pricing) that a reply must cite.
- **No scenario-pattern registry.** After a watch run, `in:draft` held **3 near-identical Joe Berman
drafts and 12 near-identical BIONPACT trademark drafts**. Each was individually reasonable; nothing
detected that they were the same matter repeated. §6.6's pattern gate needs a keyed registry to be
enforceable rather than aspirational.
- **Enforcement is documentary.** §6.1–§6.4 are not yet machine-checked. Natural attach points are
  the `first_contact` gate and tier classifier in `app/email_inbox_watch.py`.
