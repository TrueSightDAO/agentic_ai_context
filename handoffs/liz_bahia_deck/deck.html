Itacaré — Culture & the Cacao Coast
FORRÓ · CAPOEIRA · YOGA · THE ATLANTIC FOREST
  • Forró & capoeira through the week — LahSelva (Tue/Thu 18:00; Fri "Wild Forró" 21:00), Casa Maré forró (Mon 18:00), Tribo Bahia (samba Tue, forró Wed & Sun).
  • Sunday calm — Casa Fluir Vipassana (11:00), family constellation (17:00).
  • Cachoeira do Cleandro — a waterfall reached by boat up the Rio de Contas (~30 min through mangroves from Praia da Concha); swim, lunch riverside, sunset on the way back. Half-day.
  • Capoeira + cacao: the same circle of culture, health and community we bring to the USA. Private capoeira lessons can be booked anytime (Tribo Bahia).
  • Lively, on its own terms — not a party town. Itacaré's rhythm belongs to the town: capoeira training and samba rodas run every night, for the community itself, not for visitors.
  • Quilombos & the Feira Quilombola — eight certified quilombola communities, the historic root of cacao and capoeira. The monthly fair at Porto de Trás opens with Capoeira Angola — the slow, berimbau-led lineage. The year-round Roteiro Quilombola can be arranged for our dates.
Evening programme only — daylight hours stay free for farm visits. Visits are organised in September, when the Bahia cacao harvest (safra) begins.

Where to Eat — Itacaré & the Cacao Coast
FOOD NOTES  ·  BAHIAN KITCHEN, FRESH FROM THE SEA
The dish to try first
MoquecaBahia's signature seafood stew — fish or shrimp, dendê (palm) oil, coconut milk, tomato & coriander.
AcarajéBlack-eyed-pea fritter fried in dendê oil, split and filled with vatapá, shrimp & salad. Street food, best at sunset.
Vatapá & CaruruRich Afro-Brazilian stews of shrimp, nuts and dendê — the heart of Bahian cooking.
Pão de queijoWarm cheese bread — perfect with the morning cacao.
Açaí & fresh fruitThe coast is bursting with tropical fruit, juices and açaí bowls.
Places to eat
Restaurante Tia DethItacaré — the "queen of moquecas"; watch the river go by.
Restaurante Sabores da TerraItacaré — generous regional cooking and a celebrated moqueca.
Cabana da Ximbica / AlohaBeach shacks at Tiririca — moqueca, seafood & a sunset view.
Saravá SaraváModern Brazilian cooking; relaxed, stylish.
Street acarajé standsEvening stalls in town — cheap, fresh, unforgettable.
Vegetarian and vegan: plentiful. Ask for "sem dendê", or the "moqueca de banana-da-terra" (plantain). Juice bars and açaí are everywhere.

Itacaré After Dark — Rodas & Samba Nights
CAPOEIRA RODAS · SAMBA · THE WEEKLY RHYTHM OF THE TOWN
  • Every evening, somewhere in town: a capoeira roda or a live samba. These are the weekly rhythms the locals keep — open to all, arrive as you are.
  • Capoeira spills onto the street — kids and mestres circle up and the berimbau sets the pace.
  • Samba runs late — the town's bars and patios fill with the sound of the roda and samba de raiz.
Times are local (BRT, UTC−3) and typical — confirm the day's spot with your host. Evening programme only; daylight stays free for farm visits.
