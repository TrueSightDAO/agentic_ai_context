## Supplier notes

### FATOMAQ (cracker + winnower) — Brazil-built

- Maker: **Fatomaq Máquinas** — Espírito Santo do Pinhal/SP, Brazil.
- Brazil-built → 220 V / 60 Hz native, WEG parts serviceable in Bahia, no import
duty vs China. Relevant since processing partners are in Ilhéus.
- Channels: Instagram @fatomaqmaquinasagricolas · linktr.ee/fatomaq ·
mfrural.com.br/busca/fatomaq.
- Used unit (R$ 4.500) located Florianópolis/SC, ~6 months use, freight by buyer.

### Kingma melanger — China

- Supplier: Hangzhou Kingma Ware Trading Co., Ltd. — Alibaba, 4.8/5.0 (19
reviews); main markets US, PH, JP, NL, MX; 1-yr warranty; lead ~8 days.
- **Confirm before ordering:** (a) which capacity 25/60/100 L the US$ 675 price
buys, (b) 220 V / 60 Hz variant for Brazil (many CN listings are 50 Hz).

### Scale check

- Roaster 250 g = craft / sample scale. FATOMAQ 17–23 kg/h = craft batch.
- A 4–10 L melanger (≈US$ 1.6–1.8k) is the equivalent craft tier if volumes stay
small; 25–100 L Kingma is commercial-class.
