## 1. Thesis

China's manufacturers do not lack capability — they lack **verifiable traceability**, and that gap caps their ESG ratings below the AAA level that US and European manufacturers hold, blocking EU/US market access. China is positioned to **export its digitalization**: to offer supply-chain traceability digitization as a service. The TrueSight lineage stack is the evidence layer that makes this service real — and it is **already running today in Brazil**, a BRICS member and one of China's largest trading partners.

## 2. The problem: the ratings gap

- US/EU manufacturers hold top (AAA) ESG ratings because their evidence is structured, audited, and verifiable.
- Chinese manufacturers — equally capable — are downgraded or detained (EU CSRD/CSDDD/EUDR; US UFLPA + CBP June 2026 guidance) because evidence is paper-based, fragmented, and not independently verifiable.
- The same gap affects manufacturers across BRICS and Belt & Road countries.

| Bloc | Countries | Play |
