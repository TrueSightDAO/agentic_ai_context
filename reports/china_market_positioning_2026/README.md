## Source basis

The primary source was a pair of Chinese-language screenshots of an AI (DeepSeek) analysis of store churn at 嘉荣超市, supplied by the governor. English OCR failed twice (19% / 22% confidence); a `chi_sim` re-run read them at 86% / 79%. Everything in that source was then treated as *leads*, not facts, and independently checked against CCFA rankings and local trade press (赢商网, 凤凰网广东, 新浪财经, 东莞日报).

## Headline findings

### 1. The mid-tier hypermarket band has emptied, and it emptied first

| Operator | Dongguan status | Date |
|---|---|---|
| 沃尔玛 莞城西城楼 | closed | 2019-06 |
| 百佳 (ParknShop) | last store closed | 2021-01 |
| 家乐福 (Carrefour) | **exited Dongguan entirely** | 2023-12 |
| 永辉 东城万达 | closed | 2025-07 |

The sequence matters more than the count: the mid-tier was emptying for four years **before** the discount tier arrived. Discount did not kill the Dongguan hypermarket — it moved into ground the hypermarket had already vacated.

### 2. JiaRong is contracting deliberately, not collapsing

CCFA 2024 中国超市百强: **97 supermarkets, ¥60.80亿, rank #28, sales +6.5% on store count −7.6%.** Closing stores while growing revenue is a format retreat, not a collapse.

A separate CCFA line shows ¥79亿 at rank #62 across ~140 outlets (all banners). Different scope, also genuine. The two are not contradictory and should be presented as two views.

Every closure in the source screenshots (高埗 2024-09, 化州 2025-08, 常平 2026-04, 黄江 2026-10-08) is this strategy, not a crisis.

### 3. The productivity gap is a business model, not bad management

| Operator | Sales per store | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 山姆 Sam's Club | ~¥2.2bn | REPORTED, derived |
| 嘉荣 3rd-gen | ~¥86m | ESTIMATE (uplifted cohort) |
| 嘉荣 chain average | ~¥62.7m | VERIFIED arithmetic (¥60.80亿 ÷ 97) |
| 美宜佳 | ~¥1.4m | ESTIMATE |

~4,000 SKUs on a destination trip versus ~36,000 on a top-up trip. No mid-tier operator can close this gap on its own terms.

### 4. What actually works inside JiaRong

3rd-gen store disclosures: **58.9% fresh share, 72% member sales, ~20% online, 10.5% own-brand penetration.** These are not the numbers of a failing operator. The format works; it is out-scaled.

### 5. New finding — 嘉荣荟 sub-brand

JiaRong launched a **premium mall sub-brand 嘉荣荟** at 寮步 坑口红荔路景泰香都, opened **2025-09-28**, 3万㎡, 100+ brands, positioned "年轻、时尚、潮流、品位" at young and modern-family shoppers. It claims a 15 km catchment explicitly covering 东城, 松山湖 and 大岭山.

This reframes the thesis: JiaRong is not only retreating from the mid-tier, it is simultaneously attacking the premium tier. Treat it as a two-front strategy.

### 6. The actual threat is 10 km away

超盒算NB (Hema hard-discount) opened **大朗卓为城 2026-01-23**, with ~100 further South China openings planned for 2026. That is roughly 10 km from 大岭山. A hard-discount operator that close sets a price ceiling in the channel that would otherwise absorb a ¥60–120 bar.

Also relevant: **Sam's Club Dongguan is two stores** — #1 in 寮步镇; #2 signed for 厚街镇 on 2025-04-25 (¥5.11亿 investment, 2万㎡, ~1,000 parking, targeting end-2026). Sam's is doubling down while the mid-tier exits.

### 7. Why the base town is structurally weak for premium retail

赢商网 characterises 大岭山 as still at the "百货、商业街的初期阶段", lacking large-format retail, with population flowing out to 长安 and 虎门. Dongguan is a 直筒子市 — 4 streets + 28 towns, no districts — so consumption is structurally dispersed. 美宜佳's density (37,943 nationally, 2,048 per Dongguan town/street) caps basket size by construction.

Read the local shelf as a **baseline**, not as a market.

## Implications for the 81% bar

1. The premium band (¥60–120 / 50 g) is real but thin off-Sam's. Sam's opening proved Dongguan pays premium — it did not prove the mid-tier can sell premium.
2. **SPAR membership is the one non-replicable asset** in this analysis. It is a route into import/own-brand assortment that 美宜佳 and 超盒算NB cannot match.
3. Do not anchor to a mid-tier hypermarket as a primary channel while that band contracts 7.6% a year. Treat it as a display/credibility channel, not volume.
4. Read the Sam's shelf first (寮步) — that is where the ¥99.9 每日黑巧 and Bouchard reference SKUs were photographed. Note the per-kg maths: ¥99.9 for a 15 g × 13 daily-count pack.

## Open items — NOT asserted as fact

1. **The reported 大岭山 嘉荣 closure.** This appears only as "此外，东莞大岭山店也有网友提及已倒闭" inside an AI answer — a netizen claim, which direct Chinese-language search could not corroborate. Recorded as **unknown**, not closed. One phone call settles it.
2. **Whether 超盒算NB 大朗 carries premium dark chocolate at all.** Determines whether it is a price-ceiling risk to our category or irrelevant.
3. **Whether the 领尚 anchor Walmart is still trading.** Registered address verified (领尚天地商住区第10栋集中商业地上二三层, 107国道北侧); live status not.
4. JiaRong's current total store count — 160+ vs ~140 circulate; CCFA gives 97 supermarkets only.

## Field-visit itinerary (ranked by value, not distance)

1. **山姆俱乐部 寮步** — highest priority; premium chocolate as a destination category
2. **领尚购物中心 + 沃尔玛 大岭山玉屏路分店** — local baseline, read this *first*
3. 凯东中央街 · 天和百货 — the pre-upgrade tier, still trading
4. **嘉荣荟 寮步** — can a supermarket group build a premium mall brand
5. Any surviving old-format 嘉荣 — the contrast is the finding
6. **超盒算NB 大朗** — the price ceiling, seen directly
7. 长安 (万科广场 / 万达) — where 大岭山's spending leaks
8. 松山湖 — closest analogue to a genuine premium buyer

## Shelf-audit protocol (4 checks per store)

- **A. Dark-chocolate facings by price band** — count SKUs per 50–100 g in <¥30 / ¥30–60 / ¥60–120 / >¥120. Our bar is ~¥512/kg equivalent; the band must already exist.
- **B. Where the imported set sits** — own-brand vs imported gondola vs gift box vs checkout. A destination aisle and a facing are different commercial asks.
- **C. Claim language on pack** — does *any* package print a functional claim? Photograph it if so. Prediction: none do.
- **D. Format** — single bar vs multipack vs daily-count; note g per unit and per-kg price.
