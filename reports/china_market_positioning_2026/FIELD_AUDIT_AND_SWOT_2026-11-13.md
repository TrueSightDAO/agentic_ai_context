### 1.1 Shape of the market

The distribution is a **barbell with a thin bridge**, not a smooth ladder:

```
¥113 ─┬─ dense mass-market cluster ──────────┐  (8 SKUs, ¥113–223)
      │  Dove · MM · I.MM · Lindt · Galaxy   │
      │  Ferrero · Ritter · Meiji            │
¥252 ─┴──────────────────────────────────────┘
¥300 ── sparse mid band ──────┐  (2 SKUs: Ferrero 65%, Beryl's)
¥589 ─────────────────────────┘
¥590 ── gap ───────────────────────────────────── (1,250 − 589 = 2.1× jump)
¥1250 ── single novelty item ──  (Café-Tasse 20 g)
¥1420 ── us ───────────────────────────────────── (no rung exists here)
```

- **8 of 22 SKUs sit between ¥113 and ¥223/kg.** That band is crowded and price-transparent.
- **¥300–590/kg holds exactly 2 SKUs** — thin, but occupied by brands with real claims.
- **Above ¥590/kg there is one item in the entire sample**, and it is a 20-gram €3-style impulse treat in a mall supermarket.
- **No rung exists at ¥1,420/kg.** Our bar is not over-priced against a comparable — it is *outside the observed distribution*.

## 2. Cross-channel findings

### 2.1 The same SKU, two channels, 27% apart

明治 Meiji 超纯黑巧克力 70%, 75 g — identical product, both tags photographed:

| Store | Price | ¥/kg |
|---|---:|---:|
| 超盒算NB (hard discount) | ¥14.90 | 198.67 |
| blt (premium, MixC) | ¥18.90 | 252.00 |
| **Delta** | **+¥4.00 (+26.8%)** | **+53.33** |

This is the cleanest available measurement of what **premium placement is worth** on an unchanged product in Dongguan: roughly **+25–30%**. Useful as a planning constant — premium channel access is worth approximately a quarter more revenue per unit, not a multiple.

### 2.2 Meiji ran the controlled experiment for us

Same brand, same 75 g format, same price, two origins and two cocoa levels:

| Variant | Cocoa | Origin claim | Price | ¥/kg |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 明治黑巧克力 | 50% | 多米尼加可可豆 (Dominican) | ¥18.90 | 252.00 |
| 明治超纯黑巧克力 | 70% | 委内瑞拉可可豆 (Venezuelan) | ¥18.90 | 252.00 |

**+20 points of cocoa and a different single-origin claim earned zero price premium.** Origin and percentage are *marketing*, not *value drivers*, at this tier.

This is the single most important cautionary finding for our positioning: **a Brazilian-farm origin story does not by itself justify a price step.** If provenance is to carry a premium, it has to be sold in a channel that prices provenance, to a buyer who values verification — not merely printed on a pack in a mass aisle.

### 4.4 Positioning statement

> **For** the Dongguan/Shenzhen premium shopper who already pays ¥25 for 20 g of Belgian chocolate in a mall supermarket,
> **Agroverse** is the single-origin Brazilian dark bar **whose farm of origin the buyer can look up** — not read on the pack, look up.
> **Unlike** Meiji, Ritter, Beryl's or CHOCDAY, whose origin and percentage claims end at the printed label — and one of which reports 98% while only ~60% of the bar is cocoa —

The evidence does not support a single price. It supports a barbell of our own:

| 50 g price | ¥/kg | vs Café-Tasse ¥1,250 | Reading |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| ¥59 | 1,180 | **0.94×** | Sits *below* the top rung — defensible as premium without a novelty premium |

## 5. CHOCDAY 每日黑巧 — the claim-engineering case study

Highest-value competitive intelligence in the sample. Front panel leads with **总可可固形物 ≥98%**. The back label qualifies it:

| Field | Printed |
|---|---|
| 产品类型 | **混合型巧克力制品** (mixed chocolate product) |
| 总可可固形物 | ≥98% **(巧克力部分)** — of the chocolate portion |
| 可可制品添加量 | **不低于60%** — of the whole product |
| Ingredients | 可可液块, **菊粉(益生元)**, 可可脂, 食用天然香料 |
| Per serving | 每份 4.5 g 含菊粉 **1.526 g** → inulin ≈ **34%** of the bar |

**A shopper reads 98%; the product is ≥60% cocoa with roughly a third inulin.** Fully disclosed, therefore legal. It is also the clearest demonstration of why a **verifiable provenance claim is structurally stronger than a compositional percentage claim**: a percentage has a denominator that can be reweighted, a named farm does not.

Secondary finding — **origin of manufacture:** CHOCDAY's investor material describes it as using "Swiss-made" chocolate, while the physical pack in Dongguan reads 产地 **中国**, 生产商 **江苏九如食品有限公司金沙湖分公司**, under a 委托方/受委托方 structure with SC licence numbers and GB/T 19343. The pack is primary evidence. Flagged as a tension, not an allegation.

**Structural note:** that same 委托方/受委托方 arrangement — a brand-holding company contracting manufacturing and a separate cold-storage packer for 分装 — is a **working, printed template for the OEM route** (Route B).

## 6. Results against the shelf-audit protocol (README checks A–D)

### A. Dark-chocolate facings by price band

The protocol asked for SKUs per 50–100 g in `<¥30 / ¥30–60 / ¥60–120 / >¥120`. Field reality: the channels price in **¥/kg**, not per-bar, and pack sizes vary 20 g–888 g, so the per-bar bands do not partition cleanly. Reporting both:

| Per-50 g equivalent | Count | SKUs |
|---|---:|---|
| < ¥10 | 9 | Dove ×3, MM ×3, I.MM, Lindt, Galaxy |
| ¥10–12.5 | 5 | Bouchard, Meiji ×2, Ferrero, Ritter |
| ¥12.5–20 | 2 | Ferrero 65%, Beryl's |
| ¥20–30 | 3 | Beryl's 99%, CHOCDAY, Meiji CBS |
| **> ¥30** | **1** | **Café-Tasse (¥62.50 per 50 g)** |
| *Ours @ ¥71* | — | **above every band** |

**Our bar is ~14% above the highest per-50 g price in the entire sample, and the only item in its band is a 20 g novelty.**

### B. Where the imported set sits — answered

Four distinct merchandising treatments observed:

1. **Destination aisle** (blt aisle 28) — Japanese and German imports at eye level, ESL-priced, overhead category signage. This is where a craft bar belongs.
2. **Own-brand adjacent** (Sam's) — Member's Mark and I.MM taking primary position with 天天低价 category signage.
3. **Gift/boxed** (Sam's) — Rabitos, Ballotin, 音乐盒 at ¥49.90–129.90.
4. **Promotional endcap** (超盒算NB) — discount-led, price-first.

**Implication:** a destination-aisle facing and a checkout facing are different commercial asks at different costs. The blt aisle is the correct target.

### C. Claim language — **PRIOR PREDICTION FALSIFIED**

> Prior brief, check C: "does *any* package print a functional claim? Photograph it if so. **Prediction: none do.**"

**Falsified.** CHOCDAY prints a full stack, including a **regulated function claim**:

> 高膳食纤维 · High Dietary Fiber · **益生元++** · 23 kcal/份 · Clean Label 清洁标签通过华测检测权威验证 · **膳食纤维有助于维持正常的肠道功能** · 膳食纤维是低能量物质

And origin claims are mainstream, not absent: Meiji prints **委内瑞拉可可豆使用** and **多米尼加可可豆使用**; Beryl's prints **含99%可可**. The base rate for on-pack claims in this market is far higher than predicted. A new entrant with no claim would look *less* credible, not more.

### D. Format — answered

| Format | Example |
|---|---|
| Single bar | Ritter 100 g, Meiji 75 g |
| Multipack / bowl | Dove 14 g × 18 |
| Bulk bag / club | I.MM 500 g, Bouchard 504 g, Lindt 888 g |
| Daily-count small | CHOCDAY 27 g, Café-Tasse 20 g |
| Boxed gift | Ballotin 250 g, 音乐盒 338 g |
| Novelty / cereal | 卡卡星座 30 g |

**Note:** the two highest ¥/kg items in the sample are also the two **smallest** (27 g and 20 g). Premium in this market is currently expressed as *small unit, high ¥/kg* — which is a gifting/impulse grammar, not a bar grammar. Our 50 g format is larger than both.

## 11. Method note

- **Vision:** Gemini 3.8 Flash (`gemini-2.5-flash` was not provisioned on the key), strict-JSON extraction, temperature 0, images downscaled to ≤1600 px. Back labels re-run at 1.6× upscale across 4 rotations and the highest-yield rotation retained.
- **OCR baseline** (Tesseract) was unreliable on these photos — 0.0–60.9% confidence, frequent total failure — and was used only for triage, never as the source of a price or a percentage.
- **Deduplication:** all 80 images md5-checked; 0 exact duplicates. The two Per Piacere shots are the same pack photographed at 90° and 270°.
- **EXIF:** **0 of 80 images carried GPS**, and none carried camera Make/Model or DateTime. Every photo arrived via Discord. Store attribution therefore rests on storefront signage and receipts.
- **PDF:** intentionally not produced here. The existing build chain notes that ReportLab's default Helvetica silently drops CJK glyphs (嘉荣 blank, 亿 → `I`) while Latin text looks correct — so any PDF must go through the python-docx → LibreOffice path and be verified with `pdftotext | grep` on the CJK strings.
