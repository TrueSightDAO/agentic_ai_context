Source of record for `Amazon_Partnership_Landing_Page_Brief.pdf`, delivered in Discord `#cacao-innovation-center`. Companion document to `HAKEM_INTERNAL_MEETING_BRIEF.md` — same underlying facts, written for an external marketing audience instead of internal meeting prep.

## Purpose and audience

A landing page introducing the partnership story to Jonathan Hakem and the people he introduces us to — likely investors, operators, and mission-aligned contacts in the regenerative-agriculture and quantified-health space. This is a relationship and credibility page, not a product checkout page.

Everything in "What you can say" is accurate today and can be written with confidence. Everything in "What not to claim yet" is a hard guardrail — each one is there because the claim is either unverified or not yet true, and the audience includes people capable of checking.

## The core narrative

- **Regenerative** — cabruca and agroforestry systems. Cacao grown under intact forest canopy, not cleared land.
- **Transformative** — what the premium does to a farmer's economics, not just what's in the bean.
- **Transparent** — a QR code on every bag resolves to a named farm, lot, and manager. Real, running today, the single strongest thing on the page.

Then the method: how we blueprint cacao. Lead with the process (spec → measure → publish → drop what fails), not a single claim.

## What you can say, with confidence

- A working per-unit lineage system — QR resolving to farm, vintage, lot, manager. Per bag, not per brand.
- Two distinct growing regions through two cooperative partners — Bahia cabruca and Pará agroforestry.
- A partnership with a leading accredited testing authority in Brazilian cacao research.
- Real, working export experience: documented customs clearance, food-safety filings, compliance pathways already in motion for both regions.
- A QR-traceable tree-planting program tied to specific, verifiable planting events — each resolves to a real plot, not a generic counter.

## Positioning angles, and how to frame each one safely

**Coffee replacement / sustained energy** — frame as a lower-acid, naturally energising alternative to coffee, never caffeine-free (cacao genuinely contains caffeine and theobromine).

**Bioactive / "max activeness"** — no specific number or superlative until a lab figure exists. Frame around process (single-origin, minimally processed) not a named compound or percentage.

**One tree per sale / planting commitment** — do not state as a blanket universal promise. Frame the program as real and traceable ("every planting event is logged to a specific plot and publicly viewable") without committing to a 1:1 ratio for every unit sold.

**ESG / impact positioning** — fine as a category signal. Do not imply a certification, rating, or measured carbon/biodiversity outcome exists. Describe the practices and let the reader draw the ESG conclusion.

## What not to claim yet

- A specific flavanol, epicatechin, or any other compound concentration or percentage.
- That beans or bars are individually lab-tested ("individually batch tested") — no panel has been run yet.
- That a concentrated extract or supplement product exists or is available.
- Any specific carbon offset, biodiversity credit, or sequestration number.
- A formal ESG certification, rating, or third-party audit.
- The name of the testing lab or any specific lab partner.
- "One tree planted per sale" as a guaranteed ratio across the whole catalogue.
- That coffee replacement means caffeine-free.

## Tone and voice

- Specific over superlative. A real number, date, or place beats an adjective every time.
- Process over certificate. Show the method rather than asserting the result before it exists.
- Quiet confidence. The lineage system and cooperative partnerships are strong enough to stand plainly stated.
- No DAO, ledger, blockchain, or crypto terminology — the audience is mission/health-positioning, not crypto-native.

## Suggested page structure

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Opening | The three-word narrative + a single concrete image or fact — a place, a date, a farm name. |
| The method | How the sourcing is "blueprinted" — spec, measurement, publication. The credibility section. |
| The proof | The lineage system in action — what scanning a QR code shows. The two growing regions and their cooperative partners. |
| The partnership ask | What this page is for — introduce the mission, invite a conversation. No hard commercial CTA. |
| Close | Restate the three words. No urgency language. |
