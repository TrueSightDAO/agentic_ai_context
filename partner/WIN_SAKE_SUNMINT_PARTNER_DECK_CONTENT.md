## Slide 3 — How SunMint Works

- SunMint provides farmers with **upfront financing for high-quality cacao trees** + tools to **track planting and management** + **fair revenue** from the harvest.
- Tree financing is funded by **merchandise sales** — every product sold plants trees.
- Farmers & community handle **carbon-credit documentation, certification, and compliance**, so the impact is real and verifiable.
- Tree-planting events are documented with **photos, geolocation, and species** (Trees Registry).

Reference implementation: **Prism Percussions (`PP1`)** — Oregon drum maker. Every partner brand gets:

**Rule:** 1 pledge = 1 tree. Simple, auditable, brandable.
